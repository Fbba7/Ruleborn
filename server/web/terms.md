# Ruleborn: terms of use

Version: draft-2026-09-29

These terms are between you and the operator of Ruleborn named on the support page (/support). By playing you accept them. The privacy policy (/privacy) says what the game keeps about you and why.

## Who may play

Ruleborn is for players aged 13 and over. When the game first starts it asks your age band, not your birthdate. If you are under 13 no account is made, and an account that turns out to belong to someone under 13 is deleted at once, as the privacy policy describes. Players aged 13 to 17 get extra protections, which the game applies by age band: what lists of islands they see, when notifications arrive, and how much can be spent in a month.

## Your account

Your account is made from your platform's own sign-in: Game Center on iPhone, Google on Android. Keep that sign-in to yourself. You can delete your account at any time from inside the game; if you play with a Google sign-in, you can also delete it on the web at /account/delete. The privacy policy says what deleting it removes and what it keeps.

## What you make

Islands you build and publish, whether as your own defence or to the Island Workshop, stay yours. You let the operator store them, show them to other players, let other players play and remix them, and keep them after you withdraw them where the privacy policy says so. The Workshop asks you to clear an island yourself before you publish it. Islands are made of the game's own pieces: there is no uploading of images or other files, and island names are chosen from the game's word lists.

## Fair play

Play the game as it is served to you. Do not modify the game, automate play, read what the game hides from you, or exploit a fault. Every heist is checked by the server against the same simulation your phone runs, and a heist that does not check out is not paid. Do not build or name islands to harass, threaten or demean anyone.

## Reports, blocks and moderation

You can report a Workshop island and block another player from inside the game. A person reviews every report within 24 hours. The operator may remove an island that breaks these terms and will tell its creator why. Players who break these terms repeatedly or seriously may be banned; a banned player's islands leave matchmaking and the Workshop.

## Purchases

Everything sold is cosmetic, or the season track's premium rewards. No purchase buys power, speed or skipped waiting, and there is no paid randomness of any kind. Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play and refunded under their rules; a refunded item is taken back. Players under 18 have a monthly spending cap.

## Changes

When these terms change, the game shows the new version and asks you to accept it before you publish, play, rate or remix Workshop islands again. Reporting an island never waits for it.

## Contact

The support page (/support) gives the operator's contact address. Questions about these terms, a report, or a request about your data can be sent there.
