Delete your Ruleborn account

Deleting your account takes you out of the game at once: your sign-in, your islands in matchmaking and the target lists, your friends, invites and challenge links, your places in constellations and crews, your notifications and settings, and the game statistics kept under your pseudonym.

What stays, under an id that leads to no one: the records of heists, Renown, purchases and the ledger, which are never edited. Your Workshop islands stay listed and playable with no creator shown, unless you choose to withdraw them below.

On Android, sign in with the Google account you play with. On iPhone or iPad, delete from inside the game, on any Apple device signed in to your Game Center account. If you cannot, write to the contact address on the game's store listing.

Your account is deleted.
