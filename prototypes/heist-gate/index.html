Ruleborn

You are a Keeper. Your crew of three lands on someone else's floating island, which runs on its own magic rules, and tries to reach the vault and get out before the clock ends.

Nothing is destroyed and nobody is hurt. A crew that is caught wakes up at home. This is a test build: rough shapes, no sound, about four minutes.

Before you land

Three things to know

Tap the ground to send your crew there. Tap a crew member below the board to move just that one.

Guards see in cones. The cone fills toward you as a guard notices you. Tall grass hides you. Sneaking is slower and quieter.

The island has rules. They are named at the top left, and they apply to the guards as much as to you.

You will place three pieces of terrain, one guard and one rule. Then a crew will raid what you built and you will see how it went.
