Should the Lantern Glare stay a realm-wide alarm, be limited to one per realm, send every guard to investigate its tile, or Alert only the guards within 4 m of it?

§5.2 budgets defence by counting wards, but a Glare Alerts every guard in the realm within about half a second, so a Keeper's budget really buys Glares. On the harness (n=400), one Glare lifts realm 3's hold from 36.2% to 78.0%, and two shut it at 99.0%. One ward that decides the outcome breaks §13.2's rule that no single layer does. The register asks for the check to run over the kits a Keeper can build, not only over the generator's mix, and that check also shows ward count is not a steady dial for any kind: three Gravity Wells hold 88.8% and six hold 32.0%.
