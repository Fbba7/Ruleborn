## How it was looked at

- The board at phone size (390 points wide, one pixel a point), in six
  slices, normal and high contrast; full-resolution crops of the enamel card
  and the hero icons.
- The value step (rubric quality 8, pass item 3) measured with
  `tools/ui_gate/checks.py value-steps` on `board.png` and `board_hc.png`,
  with each control's rectangle read from `board.html` in Chromium (every
  `.button-type`, `.glass`, `.gold-round` and the selected enamel card).
- `art/ui/board/studio.html`'s materials and lights, read in the source.
- `kit-spec.md` sections 1–6 read in full; each colour of section 2.1
  compared with §21.2 and §21.8.

## Interim rulings, until the two tasks merge

1. **Red and green words on a dark surface.** Use §21.8's #FF9AAA (red, bold)
   and #3FBF7F (green) where they meet 4.5:1 and the perceptual check at
   their worst point; where they do not (on glass over the day sky, green on
   the card), carry the word on §21.8's fill instead: paper on #B3203A for an
   alarm, ink on #3FBF7F for a success. No lighter tint until the owner
   decides it.
2. **Menus' backdrop.** The key frames use §21.2's #171046 to #2C3AA8 on
   menus; the radial of the designs' sub-screens is the decision brief's
   question.
3. **The card's foot.** #1A1134, as §21.8.
4. **Icons in the key frames.** The board's four models in the toon look
   once P0-AIA-board-3 lands; before then a key frame may place the current
   bake, and its label at O3 is held to quality 3, pass item 5.
