## How it was looked at

  high contrast, over the enamel, wood and leather, studio and icon plates;
  the enamel cards and the four hero icons at full resolution.
- The value step (rubric quality 8, pass item 3) measured with
  `tools/ui_gate/checks.py value-steps` on `board.shot.json` and
  `board_hc.shot.json`, whose control rectangles the board now records.
- `art/ui/board/studio.html`'s material and shader read in the source, and
  `check_board.mjs`'s new toon check (it fails a planted
  `MeshStandardMaterial`).
- `kit-spec.md`'s diff in 36b797fd read in full. Every contrast figure the
  revised rows quote was recomputed (WCAG ratio and APCA Lc, bold text on
  its ground): all match.
