# T6 Realm screens — track notes

Branch `claude/3d-screens-world`. Owns the realm, scout, curation, builder, planner, observatory and defences
screens, `client/world/overlay_*.gd`, `client/world/stage_*.gd` and `client/tests/shots/screen_*.gd`.

Work in progress; this file is rewritten at each push.

## What the screens draw now

| Screen | 3D |
|---|---|
| Realm view | The realm's island full-bleed (`StageView`, home camera at 35°), buildings at their levels from `BuildingKit` on the island's footprints (`StageTown`), workers at their producers, pylons with their laws pinned, level badges; tap a building for its card. |
| Curation | The draft island with the defence overlay (`OverlayDefence`): open tiles as rounded plates, the picked post gold with its sight as `seen()` gives it, its moves painted green/red, a ghost guard, numbered post pins and ward shape pins. Tap a post to pick it, a green tile to move it. |
| Scout | A bare island of the target's realm level under mist; the guards it counted lined up by role on the near south rim, the wards by kind on the east rim, counts pinned; its laws named over the realm. Vault hidden: nothing placed (§12.4). |
| Observatory | Each card leads with a picture of its universe from one shared offscreen `StageView` (`StageThumbs`): the Heart and its outer realms linked by plank bridges. |
