# T6 Realm screens — track notes

Branch `claude/3d-screens-world`. T6 owns these files:

- the realm, scout, curation, builder, planner, observatory and defences screens (`client/scripts/*_screen.gd`);
- `client/world/overlay_*.gd` and `client/world/stage_*.gd`;
- `client/tests/shots/screen_*.gd`, with the shots' helpers `shot_kit.gd` and `stage_fixtures.gd`;
- `client/tests/stage_tests.gd`.

## What was done

Before this track, only the builder drew its realm. It used a `WorldView` inside a column of plates. The other six
screens were words and lists ("in words until the world draws it", TD.19). Now each of the seven screens draws its realm
in 3D, full-bleed behind its panels, on §21.1's home camera: orthographic, 35° pitch, 45° yaw. Every word, button,
test hook and screen-reader description the screens had is still there. The words sit on a sheet over the world.

### Shared pieces (`client/world/`)

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `stage_view.gd` (`StageView`) | Extends T5's `WorldView`, so the world is the heist's own. It frames the grid in whatever part of the screen the panels leave open: it reads how much the panels cover at the top and bottom (`cover_top`, `cover_bottom`) and takes `fill` and `look_up`. It tells a tap from a swipe, and a sideways swipe turns the camera a quarter (off in the builder, where a drag paints). The camera breathes a few centimetres, unless reduced motion is on. It adds a `layers` node for the screens' own pieces and `show_vault(false)` for a scout. |
| `stage_screen.gd` (`StageScreen`) | Mounts a `StageView` between the frame's sky and its column, with a pins layer above it. The column's containers let a tap fall through to the world. Its `window` is the empty stretch of column where the world shows, and its `sheet` is the words' scroll, given a share of the height. It also makes the Observatory's night sky, with its stars and the galaxy's band, generated in code. |
| `stage_realm.gd` (`StageRealm`) | A realm that is not a heist is drawn as a heist at tick 0 on the island bytes the screen holds (`RulebornReplay.start(seed, ticks, crew, island)`, the way the builder and heist do it). It also makes a bare island for a realm level, merges the island's buildings with the universe view's levels, and lifts the buildings off the island it hands the world, so the plain house never stands inside the levelled one. |
| `stage_town.gd` (`StageTown`) | The realm view's buildings. Each comes from `BuildingKit` at its level on the island's footprint, and all of them make one inked mesh. Workers stand at their producers' doors, by role, with a hop and a lean on their own beats. Chimney smoke rises, and a tapped building gets a gold ring at its foot. |
| `overlay_tiles.gd` (`OverlayTiles`) | The diorama's `DF` overlay, laid on the simulation's own tiles rather than its 9 × 9 grid of 3.9-unit cells. The home camera's 45° yaw gives the same diamond on screen. Open tiles are rounded plates (the diorama's canvas `tile` texture, generated in code). Painted tiles mark sight and moves. The cursor is edged gold where a tap acts and red where the island refuses it. A ghost of the piece bobs before it is placed. Each layer is one mesh. |
| `overlay_defence.gd` (`OverlayDefence`) | The defence overlay for curation and the defence log. It shows the plates where a post may stand and the picked post in gold. The picked guard's sight is drawn hard-edged from `RulebornReplay.seen` (§21.12), through `WorldView.selected_guard`. Its four moves are painted green where `CurationPresenter.moved` allows them and red where it does not, and a ghost guard stands on the tile it faces. Posts carry their number from the list, and wards carry their school's shape (§20.8: by number and shape, never colour alone). |
| `overlay_pins.gd` (`OverlayPins`) | Pins projected from the world every frame, each hanging by its tip: level badges, law names, post numbers, counts. |
| `overlay_sheet.gd` (`OverlaySheet`) | Dresses a screen's `UI` panels as a game's interface (§21.8). Panels are parchment with an ink outline (`#1A0C14`) and a hard drop. Buttons become chunky keys with a heavy bottom edge that sinks when pressed. The title is a ribbon, the keys sit on a night dock, and chips are bold. Fills stay `Palette`'s where words are measured, so the contrast checks still hold, in high contrast too. |
| `stage_scout.gd` (`StageScout`) | What a scout knows (§12.4: laws, guards by role, wards by kind, nothing placed). The island is drawn at the target's realm level, every tile veiled and drifting mist over it. The guards it counted stand by role along the near south rim, and the wards by kind along the east rim, with the counts pinned. The laws fly as banners at the near corner. The Vault is hidden. |
| `stage_plan.gd` (`StagePlan`) | The planner's universe as the card tells it. On a single realm, every edge tile is marked for landing. Across a universe, the outer realms hang in a column behind the Heart on plank bridges, each numbered as the words name it, with landing marks round their rims. Faults show in the alarm's colour, and chevrons run along each crossing toward the Heart. |
| `stage_thumbs.gd` (`StageThumbs`) | The Observatory's pictures. One shared offscreen `StageView` draws each card's universe in turn: the Heart at its realm level, bare, and its outer realms on bridges, with the faults the card names. Its viewport is taken out of its container so that it draws while unseen, and has a clear background. Each frame is copied to an `ImageTexture` and laid over the card's own starry night. A list of six cards costs one 3D viewport. |

### Tests

`client/tests/stage_tests.gd`, registered in `client/tests/run.gd`, checks:

- buildings stand at their levels and are lifted off the island;
- the defence overlay finds the picked guard and paints the post's moves;
- the scout draws only the rim and nothing on a tile, with the Vault hidden;
- the builder's ghost probe changes nothing, and Undo restores the island;
- the Observatory uses exactly one viewport.

The existing realm, curation, observatory, planner, defences and builder tests pass unchanged. The large-text check (§20.8 at 200%) found the night dock 12 px too wide in German, which is fixed.

## After the first integration (`da71550`)

The integrator merged all the tracks. The integrated branch is merged into this one: a merge commit, not a rebase.

- **Gesture names.** T5's `WorldView` gained its own `tapped(at)`, `TAP_SLOP` and gesture handling, which clashed with `StageView`'s members of the same names. In the integration the integrator removed `StageView`'s copies. The realm and curation screens now connect `view.tapped` and read the tile with `tile_at`.
- **Steady camera.** `StageView` now frames inside `WorldView._frame_camera`, so a tap that redraws the realm never sees the camera between the two framings. A test caught that bug: in the builder, a drag after a tap painted the wrong tiles.
- **Pinch zoom.** `StageView` scales the framing by the pinch zoom instead of overriding it.
- **Builder taps.** With `taps_zoom = false`, the builder turns off `WorldView`'s double-tap zoom, so two tiles tapped in a row are two edits.
- **Drag in the builder.** A one-finger drag only marks a gesture in `WorldView`. It neither pans nor turns the camera, and the builder paints with it. `StageTests` checks this.
- **Vault tag.** T5's VAULT tag gave away where a scouted realm's Vault stands. A `vault_shown` flag in `fx_anchors.gd` hides it on the scout, the planner and the Observatory's pictures.
- **Stills.** The `*-integrated.png` stills show the screens on T2's island and light, T3's kit and buildings and T8's law effects. The realm view now reads close to the design's still, with:
  - painted grass and timbered houses;
  - lit windows;
  - a cobbled path;
  - the levelled Star Well and its badge.

## Still worse than the design

- **Materials and kit** belong to T2 and T3. Since the integration, the ground, buildings and light are theirs and close to the design. The camera on these screens frames the whole grid where the design crops in tight, so each building reads smaller than in the design's still.
- **Characters** are T1's stand-ins (`ActorBuilder`).
- **The realm view is less alive than the design's.** The design has walkers on paths, birds, glows and banners. Here the workers hop at their doors and the chimneys smoke, but no one walks between buildings: the roster does not say who is idle in which realm.
- **Sheets.** The words are long. Even on a shorter sheet they take about a third of the screen where the design shows almost none. A collapsible sheet (a handle, or tap the world to fold it) would give the world the whole phone.
- **Scout.** The design's scout still shows the town fully placed. Ours shows mist, as §12.4 requires, so it cannot match that still by design. The mist could be richer, for example drifting layered cards with a shader edge.
- **Observatory thumbnails** are bare islands: the card gives no buildings and no realm type. A thumbnail would read better with the realm type's palette once the server's card carries it, and with a faint orbit ring like the design's.
- **Budgets** are not yet measured on these screens. Each view adds a handful of draw calls to the world: town 1, smoke 1, ring 1, overlay plates 2–3, ghost 1, mist 1, banners 1, plan marks 1. The thumbnails render one viewport, in sequence.

## Hooks in other tracks' files

- `client/tests/run.gd`: one line registers `StageTests`.
- `client/world/fx_anchors.gd` (T5): `vault_shown`, a flag that gates `_draw_vault`. It is three lines, and nothing changes unless a screen sets it.
- `client/i18n/strings.csv`: one row, `BUILDER_UNDO`.
- `WorldView` (T5) is used only through subclassing and its public members. `StageView.show_vault` reads the parent's `_vault`, and `StageThumbs` moves the view's `viewport` out of its `container`. If T5 renames these, the two places to update are `stage_view.gd` and `stage_thumbs.gd`.

## After the second integration (`d694b73`), on resuming

- **Law effects (T8's `LawFx`).** Since the third integration (`282e149`), `WorldView` attaches `LawFx` itself on every realm it draws. That covers the realm view, curation, the defence log and the builder. `StageView` dropped its own copy, whose `law_fx` member clashed with the parent's. The scout and the planner call `show_bare_laws`. It lays the laws' signatures over a bare realm and hides their crowns and the threads between pylons, which would stand on tiles they cannot know.
- **T1's characters.**
  - Workers are their own kind's `CreatureModel`, playing the design's `work` clip.
  - At the realm's zoom a worker drops its ink pass, a line too small to see that would double its triangles.
  - The scout's counted guards stand as their role's kind (`Cast.KIND`) in the guard form.
  - The guards on the realm, curation and defence screens already come from `WorldView.figure_maker`, in the guard form.
- **Folding the words.** A handle above the sheet ("Hide details"/"Show details", new strings in every locale) folds the words away, and the island reframes into the room they leave. The choice holds from screen to screen. It is on the realm, scout, curation, planner and defence screens. `StageTests` checks it, together with the realm view's law signatures.
- **Scout mist.** The mist is now soft cards that face the camera (`stage_mist.gdshader`, one mesh). Each has rounded lobes, lit lighter on top and violet beneath, and drifts unless reduced motion is on. The planner's Heart and the Observatory's pictures wear the same mist, for the same reason: they know a realm only by its counts.
- **Framing.** The realm view centres halfway toward its town. The panels' cover is measured from the sheet's handle.

### §21.3 budgets on each screen's camera

These are measured by `ShotKit`, which prints the world viewport's triangles and draw calls, at 1080 × 2340 on the fixtures.

| Screen | Triangles | Draw calls |
|---|---|---|
| Realm view | 95,000 | 104 |
| Curation | 72,000 | 95 |
| Defence log | 71,000 | 92 |
| Builder | 65,000 | 85 |
| Planner | 59,000 | 82 |
| Scout | 50,000 | 59 |
| Observatory picture | 22,000 | 28 |

- **Draw calls** are under 150 on every screen.
- **Triangles are over budget.** Every screen but the Observatory picture is above §21.3's 40,000, and the realm view is about 2.4 times it. A per-node breakdown of the realm view:

  | Part | Triangles | Owner |
  |---|---|---|
  | Buildings | 6.5k, 13k with their ink pass | `BuildingKit` (T3) |
  | Three workers | about 6k each since the cut; about 11.5k each before it | `CreatureModel` (T1) |
  | Island and scatter | about 16k | T2 |
  | Kit pieces | many 1–6k meshes | T3 |
  | Guards | about 8k | T5's figures |
  | Law effects | a few thousand | T8 |

- **What T6 drew itself:** cutting the workers' ink pass took the realm view from 112k to 95k. T6's overlays, plates, pins, smoke and mist add under 2k.
- **What would close the rest:** character LODs at realm zoom (T1), lower-detail kit and island at the home camera (T2/T3), and the guards' ink pass at realm zoom (T5).
