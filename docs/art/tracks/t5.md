# T5 Heist: the heist, the replay and the result

Branch `claude/3d-screens`. Owns `client/world/world_view.gd`, `actor_builder.gd`, the new `client/world/fx_*.gd`, and the heist, HUD, replay and result screens. T6 (`claude/3d-screens-world`) owns the other realm screens and calls `WorldView`'s public API, which stays additive.

## What is built

**The heist screen** (`client/scripts/heist_screen.gd`, `heist_hud.gd`): the world fills the screen and the HUD floats over it, as §21.8 and the Screens v3 heist lay it out.
- Top: the law banner (one chip per standing pylon, with its school's shape), the clock in a Lilita One pill counting down and turning red in its last 30 seconds, the alarm as six bells (amber to three, red from four) with "Alarm N / 6", and the loot panel with its two numbers, the bags' fill and the Vault's cracking as bars.
- Bottom: the hint on a glass pill, the orders (Pause in a rehearsal, gait, Halt, Drop) as leather buttons, the crew's portraits (`FxPortrait`: face, health ring, bag, role letters on a tag, gold ring and lift when chosen, red disc when down) at the bottom left and a big red Get out at the bottom right. Left-handed mirrors both rows.
- The HUD is laid out in two columns, one hung from the top and one standing on the bottom (`FxSkin.frame`), so large text never pushes a control off the screen. Every control, string and screen-reader name is kept.
- Surfaces are §21.8's: dark glass at 78%, violet cards, parchment with brown ink, ink outlines 1.5 points (3 in high contrast), a gloss and a drop shadow (`FxSkin`, `FxGloss`). Fonts are the designs' own Lilita One and Manrope (OFL, Latin subsets, `client/world/fx_fonts/`, with the client's font behind them).

**The world** (`client/world/world_view.gd`):
- A follow camera for the heist: 50° pitch, about 12 tiles across round the chosen crew member, 8 close up, the whole realm zoomed out (§21.1). It eases after the crew, frames them a little above the middle where the HUD leaves the screen clear, and keeps the old fixed framing for every screen that sets no `follow` (T6's screens are unchanged).
- Input: a tap is `tapped` (never part of a pinch or a twist), a long press is `held` and shows every crew member's name, a double tap steps the zoom, a pinch zooms smoothly, a twist turns a quarter, a mouse wheel zooms. No action needs two fingers.
- A gentle shake (never past §21.8's 4 pixels, none with reduced motion) and §21.1's one-second swoop with a violet wipe when the crew crosses to another realm (a cut with reduced motion).
- Actors are a root the simulation places, a `Rig` the animation bobs, leans and knocks over, and a `Body` under it: a character model replaces the body alone. Figures bob with their steps, tiptoe crouched, lean running, breathe standing, lie down when down.
- Guard sight (§21.12): the shown guard's tiles as the simulation's `seen(i)` gives them, flat and sharp-edged, with a darker line round the shape; every other guard on its feet shows a thin outline of its cone. A ring under each guard in its state's colour, a pulsing gold ring under the chosen crew member.

**World-anchored HUD** (`fx_anchors.gd`): health and bag bars over the crew, a gold chevron over the chosen one, spinning stars over one that is down; over each guard a quiet pip, or a speech bubble with "?", "??" or "!" drawn as shapes, filled white, yellow, orange or red by awareness; "z" and stars over a knocked-out guard; the VAULT tag with its cracking bar; each pylon's school shape floating over it; pop-up numbers; names on a long press; the alarm as a red tint at the screen's edge that rises with the alarm and flashes on an alert (a screen-space tint, never over the middle).

**What happens** (`fx_events.gd`): each change between two readings of `RulebornReplay` is drawn, so a replay shows the same effects every time: loot grabbed (a "+N" with a coin and a spray of coins), hits, a crew member going down (stars, a shake) and being revived (a green ring and a heart), a guard knocked out ("KO" and stars) or alerted (a red ring and the edge's flash), the alarm going up, a pylon breaking (shards in its school's colour and a shock ring), a ward going down, the Vault opening (a burst of coins and a gold ring), a crossing (the swoop).

**Laws on screen** (`fx_laws.gd`): every launch law (the 24 `launch: true` rows of `sim/src/laws_table.rs`) has a world signature drawn as motes (a `MultiMesh` moved on the CPU, one draw call) and, for most, a mark on the ground (cracks, a hex sheen, ice, a spiral, glass panes, aurora bands, arcs, rings, kinship's live green threads between neighbours), plus its movement signature bending the figures' steps (rate, hop, float, lean). Glowing dark dims the sun and the sky's light. Reduced motion slows the motes and drops the leaning.

**The replay** (`replay_screen.gd`): the same full-screen world and HUD, the camera following the crew, playback controls in the heist's skin, a tap on a guard shows its sight.

**The result** (`result_screen.gd`): the island after the heist, the replay played to its end and seen whole from the home pitch, swaying slowly, with coins flying from the island into the outcome card; the outcome on parchment with the grade in Lilita One.

## Still worse than the design

- The ground and the island are T2's and T3's: still a pale checkerboard here.
- The figures are the diorama's chibis at its 2.2 scale; T1's `CreatureModel` replaces the `Body`.
- No Sage, cast or ability portraits: the build's heist has no abilities yet, so the portraits show health, bag and state.
