# T7 Character screens

Branch `claude/3d-character-screens`. Owns the Character, Roster, Sage hall, Forms, Hatch and First-launch screens, `client/world/stage_hero*.gd`, `client/world/portrait*.gd` and `client/tests/shots/screen_*.gd`.

## What was built

- **`StageHero`** (`client/world/stage_hero.gd`): a small 3D stage for one character or a few side by side. Each stands on a patch of its school's realm (a floating island from `RealmPalette`), or all share one island (`"island"`) or the Sage hall's floor and arches (`"hall"`). The light is §21.3's three points: a warm key with shadow, the night's violet as the fill (shade never goes black), and a rim in the school's colour. Behind it, `StageHeroBackdrop` draws the menus' night with a glow in the school's colour, slow rays and rising motes. It is 2D, so it adds no 3D draw calls and needs no post-processing. The figures sway round the three-quarter view and breathe. A drag spins them, and a tap plays an action (hop, twirl, squash). `reveal()` is the hatch moment: the shell bursts in two, a ring of light flares, and the newborn pops out with one overshoot. Reduced motion stills it all.
- **`StageHero.make_figure(kind, look, close)`** uses T1's `CreatureModel` for every kind T1 has exported. `claude/3d-characters` is merged with all 43 kinds exported, so every character on these screens is the design's model. The export names kinds without underscores, and the stage maps to them. The model sits in a node the stage owns, so the stage's sway and hop don't fight its facing. Forms use `set_form`, the guard form uses `set_guard`, and a single hero stage asks for the fur shells. A tap plays the ability clip and then hands back to idle. `StageHeroSculpt` is kept only as the fallback for a kind the export lacks. Its own test still checks every stand-in against the 6,000-triangle budget.
- **`StageHeroSculpt`**: until T1 lands, a stand-in for all 24 kinds (18 ruleborn and 6 Sages), plus the Nursery's egg. Each has its body and size from §21.6, its colours, and its school's shape as a marking. It follows the cuteness rules: a big head, eyes low and wide with catch-lights, short limbs and rounded tips. Each kind is one vertex-coloured mesh inside 3,000–6,000 triangles, drawn in `toon.gdshader`. Forms change the pattern toward the note's colour, make the marking glow, and add one accessory: a crescent for Hush, a crown of sparks for Spark, a halo ring for Echo. The guard form adds §21.6's sash and lantern.
- **`Portrait`** and **`PortraitStudio`**: portraits are rendered from the models (§21.3) once each, in one shared offscreen viewport, and cached. Each is drawn whole at 512 px and then cut square from the top of what was drawn (`PortraitStudio.crop`): head and shoulders down to the knees, a tall thin one cut to its upper body, and a stray glint or antenna tip ignored. That way a kind's props (staffs, lamp poles) and its own height never mis-frame it. A card gets its texture at once and the picture fills in a frame later. Headless runs skip drawing. The look `"shadow"` draws a silhouette for a locked Sage or an unhatched form.
- **`StageHeroSkin`**: §21.8's surfaces for these screens:
  - menus on the night backdrop (#171046 to #2C3AA8);
  - dark violet cards with light words;
  - parchment plates with brown ink, outlined in ink #1A0C14, 3 pt in high contrast;
  - gold for the main action and leather for the rest, with a lip and a squash on press;
  - Lilita One numbers and Marcellus names. Both are OFL, in `client/world/stage_hero_numbers.woff2` and `stage_hero_names.woff2`, with the licence in `stage_hero_fonts_ofl.txt`.
  - portrait tiles on the school's colour.
- **Screens**:
  - **Roster**: the chosen character on its hero stage (a tap on a card's portrait chooses), then a grid of portrait cards, two to a row, one at large text.
  - **Character** (§23.2 screen 11): the in-game view idling on its stage with the level badge in Lilita One, then its three forms and its guard form side by side, with their names under them. Below that, the card on a dark plate, the traits on parchment, and the state, level and spends.
  - **Sage hall**: the Sages standing in their hall. The locked ones are silhouettes, and the one on guard is a step forward. Each card carries its portrait.
  - **Forms**: the chosen kind's three forms on the stage, unhatched ones as silhouettes. Each kind's card shows the three as portraits, and a tap on them chooses that kind.
  - **Hatch**: a Nursery's egg waits on the stage, and a birth plays the reveal.
  - **First launch**: a welcome scene of the Archivist with three ruleborn on one small island above the words.
- Every label, button, test hook, `meta` and screen-reader name was kept. The words are unchanged and still come from the catalogues; nothing new was added to the strings.

Render any of them with `bash tools/render/shoot.sh client/tests/shots/screen_<name>.gd out.png`. `screen_stage.gd` (KINDS, LOOKS, T) renders a stage alone, and `screen_portraits.gd` renders all 24 portraits.

## Still worse than the design

- **Colour:** under the stage's toon light, the models read darker and more saturated than the design's PBR renders. Bloomling's petals and Emberling's flame go red, and Mossbear's moss is darker. Matching that is a tuning job in T1's creature shader together with these lights.
- **The ground:** the islands are this track's own simple patches, toned a step darker than the figures. T2's island look (painted grass, rim bands, strata, roots) is not yet used under them.
- **The Sage hall's arches** are simple geometry, not T3's kit.
- **Clips don't follow the stage clock:** a still can catch a blink, and the reveal plays no celebrate clip.
- **Long text lists:** below each stage the screens are still lists of words. The design's chips, tabs and prev/next arrows need new strings, so they were left out.

## Decisions

- **Character outline:** `StageHero.INKED` defaults to §21.3 (inked, 2 px on the stage). The handoff notes say the owner had it removed, so it is one switch.
- **Fonts:** Lilita One and Marcellus are vendored next to the stage scripts because this track owns those paths. The integrator may move them to a shared fonts folder.
