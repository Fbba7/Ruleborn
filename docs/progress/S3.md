## Tried and failed
- Routing `ScreenBox` through the kit inside `screen_box.gd`: a new draw call or `StyleBoxTexture` raises the file's record, and a 10-point bar is thinner than the `bar_track` slice's 14 points. `KitBar` is the kit's thin bar (S3-R1).
- `SCREEN=character` on `client/tests/shots/screen_meta.gd`: an empty screen. The character screen's shot is `client/tests/shots/screen_character.gd`.
- `Control.get_global_rect()` for a shot record's rectangles: under the 540 × 1170 window override it is in the 1080-wide canvas, not the still's pixels (the title came out 710 points wide). Use `root.get_final_transform() * (c.get_global_transform_with_canvas() * Rect2(Vector2.ZERO, c.size))`, then scale by 390 over the still's width.
- Card (PanelContainer) entries in a shot record: `checks.py value-steps` leaves other controls' rectangles out of each ring, so a button inside a listed card has no ground left ("0 ground pixels"). List the title and the buttons only.
- An ability-card label loop that set the Kicker colour before wrapping: the ability tag also wears the Kicker variation, so the colour branch caught it, the tag was never wrapped, and at 200% in pt_BR the column ran 1256 pixels wide. Wrap the Tag first.
- Capping a wrapped label at its room's width alone: the label's own minimum width holds the room open, so words wider than the screen keep the column past it. Cap at the screen's side less the safe margin as well.
- Recording each tab segment as its own control: on the cream track, Stats and Compare read ΔL* 2, so value-steps fails them. Key frame 04 records the segmented row as one control, and so does `write_record`.
- A missing locale in a shot script: `Settings.locale` defaults to "", and `Settings.apply()` then fails with "Locale cannot be an empty string". Default it to "en" in the shot.
- The tabs at 100% en with a 32-point icon and an 8-point gap: "Account" needs 175 px of words in 170. Use a 22-point icon and a 4-point gap, and go compact below the icon, the gap and both margins.
