- `Control.get_global_rect()` for a shot record's rectangles: under the 540 × 1170 window override it is in the 1080-wide canvas, not the still's pixels (the title came out 710 points wide). Use `root.get_final_transform() * (c.get_global_transform_with_canvas() * Rect2(Vector2.ZERO, c.size))`, then scale by 390 over the still's width.
- Card (PanelContainer) entries in a shot record: `checks.py value-steps` leaves other controls' rectangles out of each ring, so a button inside a listed card has no ground left ("0 ground pixels"). List the title and the buttons only.

- Capping a wrapped label at its room's width alone: the label's own minimum width holds the room open, so words wider than the screen keep the column past it. Cap at the screen's side less the safe margin as well.
- Recording each tab segment as its own control: on the cream track, Stats and Compare read ΔL* 2, so value-steps fails them. Key frame 04 records the segmented row as one control, and so does `write_record`.
