T0.4

The ablation harness — is the defence fair?

Done from here

Built exactly as §13.4 specifies: all 32 layer combinations, credit divided by Shapley value. The hold rate lands inside §13.2's band (41.7%) and the shares do not. Guards and Response measure identically because they are the same layer — neither turns back a single raid without the other, in any of the eight coalitions — and together they are 69% of the defence against a 25% ceiling (F15). T0.6

Give the island generator laws, and measure it against the band

Done from here

Done, and it answered F9's caveat the other way. Adding laws does not rescue the hold rate — weaving the player's own laws at random makes the island weaker than weaving none (0–7% against 3–33%), because only five of the nineteen laws a rule sheet can express help the defender. A generator that reads the sign of each law recovers it (F11). Writing all 48 laws out against the rule sheet also settled §12.21's rule: 16 are parameter changes, 32 need an engine feature (F10), and 8 need a choice from the owner that a generator now has to make (F12).

T0.7

Specify the response layer — and report that the fix did not work

Done from here

F15's recommendation was to give Response an effect that works without a guard, using the two things §13.2 names and never defines: the Spire's reach and the guardian Sage's single power. Both are specified in spec/response.md, both were built into the harness, and neither changes the shares — guards and response still measure 34.2% each. Why is the useful part: a crew already leaves 47% of the exposed share behind, so taking loot off the floor costs it nothing, and a run ends at 40 seconds of §5.4's 165, so a delay costs it nothing either. Guards and response are the island's only detect-and-act chain — the other three layers work by delay, and delay reaches an outcome only through a clock with four times the slack it would need. Re-measured against what §9.6 actually pays (grade × fill), the identity breaks and the 69% does not.

T2.5

Specify the Aether and Essence sinks, and withdraw the first fix

Done from here

F17 said the prices were too small. spec/sinks.md says they are on the wrong kind of curve, which is a different fix. From day 2 to day 301 the faucets multiply by 122, 240 and 251; the prices aimed at them multiply by 1 to 88. Starlight's biggest sink outruns its faucet 12× because §10.2's building curve is geometric and never ends; nothing points a geometric curve at the other two. Not blocked, and still not yet

These look available. Starting any of them now spends work that a decision above will throw away.

  • Gameplay code — The determinism spike gates it. Anti-cheat, replay validation, free analytics, rehearsals, balance bots and the whole cost model fail together if it does not pass.
  • Character art — The form-notes decision in stage 4 changes the quantity by 3× — 144 forms against 54.
  • Detailed screen design — C1, C5 and C6 each change what several screens show.
  • Localisation — Twelve languages against a coined vocabulary is a large translation problem with no owner in the team model, and §20.6 already says two at launch (G11).
  • Claiming the name publicly — §26.9 makes trademark clearance a gate, and it has not been done.
